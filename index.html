Interactive audiovisual experiment / 2026

THE NOISE WE IGNORE explores the overlooked sounds that shape everyday urban life. Traffic, machinery, footsteps and distant voices form a constantly changing soundscape, yet familiarity makes them disappear from our attention.

Through moving images and sound-reactive typography, the project gives a visible form to what we usually hear without noticing. Each letter responds to the frequencies, rhythms and textures of its environment.

This interactive experience asks what happens when background noise becomes the subject of our attention. Sixteen moving-image sequences are paired with a shifting typographic manifesto, revealing the city as a living archive of sound.

Move the cursor across the image to create persistent horizontal signal distortions. Revisit the same area to alter its traces again. The visual interference accumulates, just as urban sounds leave impressions on our perception.
